2. Scope

This policy covers the MATCHOS apps for iOS and Android and the MATCHOS website. It explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, who we share it with, where it is processed, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

MATCHOS is social by design. Much of what you post is public, including to people who do not use MATCHOS. Please read Section 7 so you know what other people can see.

Some features are available on only one platform. Live streaming, Live Challenges, MATCHOS Coins and Super Chat are currently available only in the iOS app.

3. Minimum Age

MATCHOS is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older. Do not use MATCHOS if you are under 18.

MATCHOS does not ask for your date of birth or age, and it does not verify age or identity. See Section 21.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Account

When you complete the initial setup in the app (choosing a username, your nationality and your app language), MATCHOS creates an account for you automatically. The account is identified by a randomly generated user ID, and the sign-in credential for it is stored on your device.

We do not ask for your email address, phone number, real name or a password to create an account. Because of this, if you log out or lose your device, you will probably not be able to sign back in to the same account.

4.2 Profile

  • Username, which you choose.
  • Nationality (country), which you choose from a list during setup.
  • City (optional), which you type yourself.
  • Bio (optional).
  • Profile photo (optional).
  • Member number, which we assign to every account.
  • App language, and your matchmaking preferences: preferred region and languages.

4.3 Fitness and Activity

MATCHOS stores the fitness information you create or record in the app:

  • Battles and games: the exercise or game, HOME or GYM, the mode and rules, the rep counts entered by players, and the outcome (win, loss or draw). GYM battles also store the weight lifted. 4-player games, Chat Mode video rooms and Cardio Train Together sessions store who took part and when. Train Together also stores the machine type.
  • Progress: XP, level, ranked points, Tier, wins, losses and draws, and your world and country ranking.
  • Records you post: workout records such as manual records and stopwatch times (including duration), goals you set and achieve, and Weight Records. A Weight Record stores the exercise, the weight lifted, the reps, a required lift video, and your country and world rank at the time you posted.
  • Activity calendar and streak: which days you were active and what you did, and your daily activity streak.

Reps in MATCHOS are counted by people, not by the app. MATCHOS does not use AI, computer vision or body pose analysis, and it does not analyse your camera image.

MATCHOS does not connect to Apple Health (HealthKit), Health Connect or Google Fit. It does not collect your body weight, heart rate, body measurements or medical information, and it does not try to draw conclusions about your health from your data. The weight we store is the weight you lift, not your body weight.

Please think carefully before including health information (for example, an injury or a medical condition) in goals, posts, comments or messages. Public posts and goals can be seen by anyone (Section 7).

4.4 Content You Create

  • Moments: posts with photos or videos (including any sound in the video), captions and topics, as well as likes, comments, reposts and saves.
  • Social connections: who you follow and who follows you, friend requests and friendships, accounts you block, and the communities, groups and events you join.
  • Messages: direct messages and group or community messages (see Section 4.5).
  • Communities and groups you create: name, description, category and photo.
  • Events you host: title, description, type (in person or online), the venue or location text you type, start and end time, notes, and a cover photo. For GYM events, we also record when you confirmed you have permission to hold the event at the venue.
  • Live (iOS): stream titles, Live comments, Super Chat messages and Live Challenge votes (see Sections 9 and 10).
  • Reports you submit (see Section 13).

4.5 Messages

  • Direct messages are text only, up to 2,000 characters, between two people.
  • Group and community messages are text messages to the members of that group or community.

4.6 Purchases (iOS)

If you buy MATCHOS Coins, we receive and store purchase details from Apple. See Section 10.

4.7 Device and Technical Information

  • Push notification token and whether your device is iOS or Android (see Section 11). On Android, the Firebase software that delivers notifications also creates a Firebase installation ID.
  • Time zone: your device's time zone name (for example, "Asia/Tokyo"). We store it to calculate your daily streak, and the app sends it when it shows activity calendars.
  • Online status: while the app is open on your screen, it tells our servers that you are online.
  • Network information: our service providers process your IP address when your device connects to them. In calls, your IP address may also be visible to the other participants (see Section 8).

4.8 Location-Related Information

MATCHOS does not request access to your device's location and does not collect GPS or other precise location. MATCHOS processes the photos and videos you upload so that embedded location metadata (such as GPS coordinates) is not kept.

The location-related information MATCHOS does handle is:

  • the nationality you select and the city you type on your profile. Your city is used for the "Near me" list of people in the same city;
  • your device's time zone;
  • the venue or location text that event hosts type;
  • IP addresses, which are processed for network connections, calls and Live streaming (Sections 8 and 9).

We do not use your IP address to work out where you are.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

MATCHOS does not collect:

  • your email address, phone number or real name (you can choose to put your real name in your username or bio);
  • your date of birth, exact age, or a record of age confirmation;
  • GPS or other precise location from your device;
  • data from Apple Health, Health Connect or Google Fit, or your body weight, heart rate or medical information;
  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • your card number or other payment details (Apple handles payment);
  • how long you watch Live streams;
  • analytics or crash reports (the apps do not include analytics or crash-reporting tools);
  • recordings of Battle, video call or Chat Mode audio or video (except in a Live Challenge; see Section 9).

We do not show third-party advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • run MATCHOS: create and maintain your account; show your profile; run battles, games, calls, Live streams and events; deliver messages; and calculate records, rankings, XP, Tiers and streaks;
  • recommend content in the For You feed (Section 12);
  • process coin purchases, Super Chat and refunds, and keep the related records (Section 10);
  • send push notifications (Section 11);
  • keep MATCHOS safe: handle reports, apply Yellow Cards, review accounts, enforce our rules, and prevent abuse and fraud (Section 13);
  • protect and secure the service;
  • resolve disputes and handle legal claims, for example about a purchase, a refund or a moderation decision;
  • meet legal, tax and accounting obligations that apply to us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Public Information and User Content

7.1 Visible to anyone, including people not using MATCHOS

The following information can be read by anyone, without signing in to MATCHOS:

  • your profile: username, nationality, city, bio, profile photo and member number;
  • who you follow and who follows you;
  • your Moments, including photos, videos, captions and topics, and the likes and comments on them;
  • your goals and the workout records you post, including Weight Records and their lift videos;
  • Weight Record rankings.

7.2 Visible to signed-in MATCHOS users

  • your level, XP, Tier and ranked statistics, such as wins and losses;
  • whether you are online now, and your current and longest streak;
  • a summary of your activity calendar: the exercise or game, the mode, and HOME or GYM, but not your results, reps or opponents;
  • events, and the list of people taking part in each event;
  • communities, including their names, descriptions and member counts;
  • Live streams, Live comments and Super Chat messages;
  • a Yellow Card title on your profile, if you have received Yellow Cards.

7.3 Visible only to participants

  • Direct messages: the two people in the conversation.
  • Group and community messages: members of that group or community.
  • Detailed battle results: the people in that battle. You can see your own full history.
  • Online event access codes: the event host and the people who joined.

7.4 Media files

Moment photos and videos, Weight Record videos, profile photos, community photos and event photos are stored in public file storage. Anyone who has a file's web address can view that file without signing in.

When you delete content, we delete the stored file. However, copies may remain in content delivery network (CDN) or browser caches for a period of time, so a previously shared address may keep working for a while after deletion.

7.5 Blocking

Blocking hides people from each other inside the app and stops new interactions between you. It does not make public information private. For example, someone you have blocked can still see your public profile and Moments while signed out.

7.6 Sharing

The share button uses your device's share sheet. It shares text, or for a Battle result an image card that shows both players' usernames, scores and the result. You decide where to send it.

8. Battles and Video Calls

Battles, 4-player games, Chat Mode video rooms, Cardio Train Together and Live Challenges use real-time video and audio between participants.

  • Connections: audio and video travel directly between participants' devices where possible. If a direct connection is not possible, they are relayed through Cloudflare's relay (TURN) service. The media is encrypted in transit.
  • Connection setup: to start a call, the app exchanges technical connection details (such as network addresses and media settings) through our real-time messaging service, provided by Supabase. Your device also contacts public connection (STUN) servers run by Google, and Cloudflare's servers.
  • Your IP address: because of how these connections work, other participants in a call, Google's STUN servers and Cloudflare may see your device's IP address.
  • No recording: MATCHOS does not record or store the audio or video of Battles, 4-player games, Chat Mode video rooms or Train Together sessions.
  • What we store: who took part in a call, game or room; when it started and ended; and, for Battles, the results described in Section 4.3.

Exception: if you take part in a Live Challenge, your video and audio become part of the host's public Live stream (Section 9).

9. Live Streaming (iOS)

When you go live, the video from your camera, and your audio if your microphone is on, is sent to our streaming provider, Mux. Mux delivers the stream to viewers.

  • What we store: the stream title, its status, its start and end times, a viewer count, Live comments and Super Chat messages. We do not store your stream key.
  • What Mux receives: your live video and audio, and an internal stream identifier generated by MATCHOS. We do not send your username or user ID to Mux when creating a stream. When people watch, Mux also processes viewers' network information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the video.
  • Playback: watching a stream requires a short-lived signed access token, which we issue to signed-in users.
  • Recordings: Mux automatically creates a recording of each live stream. MATCHOS does not make these recordings available for playback. When Mux tells us that a recording is ready, our systems ask Mux to delete it. Until that deletion is complete, the recording exists in Mux's systems.
  • Live Challenges: if you join someone's Live stream as a challenger, your video and audio are sent to the host's device and included in the host's broadcast. That means they are seen by the host's viewers, sent to Mux, and included in the recording described above. Votes in a Live Challenge are stored with the voter's account. Other users see only the vote totals.
  • Watch time: MATCHOS does not record how long you watch Live streams.

Live is not available in the Android app.

10. Coins, Purchases and Super Chat (iOS)

On iOS, you can buy MATCHOS Coins through Apple's In-App Purchase.

  • Payment: Apple processes the payment. MATCHOS does not receive your card number or other payment details.
  • What we receive and store: to confirm a purchase and add coins, we receive transaction information from Apple. It includes transaction identifiers, the product, the quantity, the price and currency, the App Store country or region, and the purchase date. We also receive Apple's notifications about refunds and other changes to purchases, and we keep a copy of each notification exactly as Apple signed it.
  • Linking purchases to your account: when you buy coins, the app gives Apple your MATCHOS user ID as an account token, so Apple's purchase records and notifications can be matched to your account.
  • Coins and Super Chat: we store your coin balance and a record of coins you buy and spend. When you send a Super Chat, we store the amount, your message, who sent it and which stream received it. We also keep accounting records of coins spent on Super Chat, including which creator account received them.

Coins and Super Chat are not available in the Android app.

11. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token with your account. We use it to send notifications through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android).

  • When we send notifications: currently only for new direct messages and new friend requests. We do not currently send notifications for group or community messages.
  • What a notification contains: a generic title such as "New message" or "Friend request", plus internal identifiers so the app can open the right screen. It does not include the message text or the sender's name.
  • Android: Firebase Cloud Messaging uses your device's messaging token and a Firebase installation ID. The Firebase software may also send technical information about notification delivery to Google.
  • Turning them off: you can turn notifications off in your device settings. When you log out, the app removes your device's push token from your account. Deleting your account deletes all of your push tokens.

12. Recommendations

The For You feed recommends Moments based on how you use MATCHOS. It uses only these signals from inside MATCHOS:

  • Moments shown to you (impressions);
  • Moments you open;
  • likes and comments;
  • follows;
  • topics you open;
  • profiles you open.

It also takes into account how recent and how popular a Moment is. We store these signals as IDs and event types, and only you can read your own. We do not use your location, Live viewing, or information from other apps or websites for recommendations, and we do not use recommendations for advertising.

The Following and Recent feeds are not personalised: they show posts in time order. You can use them instead of For You at any time. You can also object to For You personalisation (Section 19).

13. Reports and Safety

  • Reporting: you can report profiles, Moments, comments, direct messages, group messages, battles and communities. A report stores who reported, who was reported, what was reported, the reason, any note you add, and a copy of the reported content at the time of the report. Depending on the content, that copy can include the reported username, a bio, post text, a media file reference or message text.
  • Who sees reports: only you can see the reports you submit. MATCHOS can see all reports. Reported people cannot see in the app who reported them.
  • Yellow Cards: reports can lead to Yellow Cards on an account, and a Yellow Card title can appear on a profile. We keep a record of which reports contributed to Yellow Cards and a log of moderation actions.
  • Moderation alerts: when an account reaches a review threshold, our systems send an email alert to the operator using Resend. The alert includes the account's username, member number, user ID, Yellow Card count, the time the account was flagged, and the reason, target type and report ID of the latest report. It does not include the reported item itself, message text, captions or media.
  • Group moderation: the host of a group or community can remove any message in it. Moderators can remove ordinary members' messages and their own. When a message is removed, its text is deleted, and a placeholder recording that it was removed, by whom and when remains.

16. Data Retention

While your account exists, we keep your profile, content, records, settings and recommendation signals until you delete them or delete your account (Section 17).

Automatic deletion. Finished matchmaking requests are deleted 30 days after they finish.

After you delete content or your account, some information is kept for the purposes below.

  • Moderation and safety records: reports (including the copy of reported content and the reported username), Yellow Card contribution records, moderation action logs, and moderation alert records and emails (including the username and member number at the time of the alert). We keep these to protect users, prevent repeat abuse, and handle disputes and legal claims about moderation decisions. They are linked to the user ID of the account involved. These records are not deleted automatically after a fixed period. Instead, we review them at least once a year and delete or anonymize records that are no longer reasonably necessary for safety, abuse prevention, dispute handling or applicable legal obligations. Records connected to an unresolved dispute, ongoing abuse or fraud, or a legal requirement may be kept for as long as that need continues. Copies of moderation alert emails are held in the operator's mailbox and in Resend's systems, and are covered by the same review.
  • Purchase and accounting records: coin purchase records, transaction identifiers, refund records, accounting records for Super Chat, and Apple's signed purchase notifications (which contain your MATCHOS user ID as the purchase account token). These are a separate category from moderation records. We keep them for at least as long as required by the tax and accounting rules that apply to us, and for as long as they are needed for refunds, disputes and fraud prevention. They are not deleted automatically after a fixed period.
  • Records no longer linked to you: some records of battles, games, rooms and sessions remain after an account is deleted, with the deleted account's link removed (for example, a battle record with no participants attached, or an opponent's ranked history with your account removed).

Our service providers may also keep technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for their own operational periods.

17. Account Deletion

In the app: go to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer use the app (for example, you lost your device), you can ask us to delete your account by emailing surf6589@gmail.com with your username and member number. Because MATCHOS accounts have no email address or password, we may ask for further information to confirm the account is yours before we delete it.

When your account is deleted, we delete:

  • your profile, settings and matchmaking preferences;
  • your Moments, comments, likes, reposts, saves, goals and records;
  • all files stored in your account's storage folder, including photos and videos;
  • your follows, friendships, friend requests and blocks;
  • your group and community messages;
  • every direct message conversation you were part of, including the other person's messages in that conversation;
  • your Live streams, Live comments, Super Chat messages and Live Challenge votes;
  • your coin balance and ordinary coin transaction history;
  • your XP, streak, online status and recommendation signals;
  • your push tokens;
  • your ordinary Yellow Card status.

You will also be removed from any active battle, room, call or session.

The information described in Section 16 under "After you delete content or your account" is kept. As described in Section 7.4, deleted files may remain in caches for a period of time.

18. Security

We use measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • access rules in the database that limit who can read each type of information;
  • encrypted connections between the app and our servers;
  • signed, short-lived tokens for Live playback;
  • processing uploaded photos and videos so that embedded location metadata is not kept;
  • contractual commitments from our service providers (Section 15).

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Because MATCHOS accounts are anonymous and the sign-in credential is stored on your device, please keep your device secure.

19. Your Privacy Rights

In the app, you can:

  • edit your profile;
  • delete your Moments (a Weight Record is deleted as a whole post, together with its video), comments and likes;
  • leave groups, communities and events;
  • block other users;
  • use the Following or Recent feed instead of For You;
  • turn camera, microphone and notification permissions on or off in your device settings;
  • delete your account (Section 17).

Some things cannot currently be done in the app:

  • You cannot delete individual direct messages. A direct message conversation is deleted only when either participant's account is deleted.
  • Ordinary members cannot delete their own group or community messages. Hosts and moderators can remove them (Section 13). Your group messages are deleted when your account is deleted. Leaving a group does not delete them.
  • There is no in-app tool to download a copy of your data, or to restrict or object to particular uses of your data.

Requests by email. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know how we handle your personal information, and get access to it or a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • have us stop or restrict certain uses of it, or object to them (including For You personalisation);
  • receive it in a portable format.

To make a request, email surf6589@gmail.com. MATCHOS accounts are not linked to an email address, so we may ask for information from the app, such as your username and member number, to confirm the account is yours. We do not charge a fee for requests. You can also contact us at the same address with any complaint about how we handle your personal information.
