Fitness that connects people.

MATCHOS is a social fitness platform. Challenge someone to a workout battle, go live, find your community, join events and train together — at home or at the gym.

What you can do on MATCHOS

  • Battle

    Workout battles

    Face another person in a head-to-head workout over video, with bodyweight battles at home or weight-based battles at the gym.

  • Live

    Live streams

    Broadcast your training, chat with viewers and take on challenges while you are live.

  • Community

    Communities

    Discover and join communities of people who train the way you do, and keep the conversation going in group chats.

  • Events

    Events

    Find and host home and gym workout events, and meet people who share your goals.

  • Train Together

    Workout partners

    Train side by side with a partner on a video call, so you never have to work out alone.
