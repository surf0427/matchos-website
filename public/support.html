MATCHOS Support

Need help with MATCHOS? Here is how to reach us.

Contact us

Email surf6589@gmail.com for help with MATCHOS. Use the same address for:

  • general support: questions about the app, your account, battles, Live, events or communities;
  • privacy requests: access, correction, deletion and other requests described in the Privacy Policy;
  • account deletion, if you can no longer use the app (see below);
  • purchase questions about MATCHOS Coins (see below);
  • questions about the Terms of Service.

A person reads every email. There is no automatic ticket system, so please send one email per issue. When you contact us, include your MATCHOS username and member number, your device model, the operating system version and a short description of what happened.

Report a problem or stay safe

  • Report content or a person in the app. Use the report option on a profile, Moment, comment, message, battle or community. Reports can lead to Yellow Cards, content removal or suspension (see the Terms of Service).
  • Block someone from their profile to hide them from you in the app and stop new interactions. Blocking does not make public information private.
  • Someone under 18? MATCHOS is only for adults. Report the account and choose the “underage” reason, or email us.
  • In danger? If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. MATCHOS cannot send emergency help.

Delete your account

In the MATCHOS app, go to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer use the app (for example, you lost your device), email surf6589@gmail.com with your username and member number to ask us to delete your account. Because MATCHOS accounts have no email address or password, we may ask for more information to confirm the account is yours.

What is deleted and what is kept is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Coins and purchases (iOS)

MATCHOS Coins are bought through Apple’s In-App Purchase, so Apple handles payment and refund requests. To ask for a refund, use Apple’s “Report a Problem” page at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Coins you paid for did not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and your username and member number. Do not send us card details. See the Terms of Service for how Coins work.
